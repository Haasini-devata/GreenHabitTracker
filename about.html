About Green Habit Tracker

Turning small daily actions into a big difference for our planet.

Our Story

Green Habit Tracker began with a simple thought: what if everyone made one eco-friendly choice each day? Over time, those small acts can create a ripple effect of positive change. From switching off unused lights to walking instead of driving, every step matters.

Our Mission

We aim to make sustainable living easy, engaging, and rewarding. By tracking habits, providing helpful reminders, and celebrating your progress, Green Habit Tracker helps you stay motivated and see the real-world impact of your efforts.

Why It Matters

Climate change isn’t just a global problem—it’s a personal one. The choices we make in our homes, workplaces, and communities shape the world we live in. By building eco-friendly habits, we can protect our environment for future generations.

Join Us

You don’t need to be perfect—just willing to start. Whether you plant a tree, reduce plastic, or simply choose to walk instead of drive, you’re part of a growing movement towards a greener future.
